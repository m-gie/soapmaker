description=[h2]Making Soap[/h2]
description=Mix [b]1 Lard + 1 Charcoal + 1 L of Water[/b] in a Cooking Pot to make [b]2 Raw Soap[/b].
description=
description=Cook the Raw Soap for [b]60 minutes[/b] in an oven, campfire, or barbecue to turn it into proper Soap Bars.
description=
description=Running low on lard? [b]Trim Fat[/b] from 2 raw cuts of pork, beef, venison, or mutton with a knife, then cook the Raw Fat for [b]60 minutes[/b] to render it into lard.
description=
description=But first, you need to know what you're doing.
