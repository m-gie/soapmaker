description=[h1]Soapmaker[/h1]
description=Never run out of soap again. Render lard from fatty meat, mix it with charcoal and water, and cook your own soap bars.
description=
description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*][b]Trim Fat[/b]: 2 raw cuts of pork, pork chop, beef, steak, venison or mutton chop plus a knife give [b]Raw Fat[/b]. Cook it for 60 minutes to get [b]Lard[/b].
description=[*][b]Mix Soap[/b]: 1 full lard, 1 charcoal and 1 L of water in a cooking pot give 2 [b]Raw Soap[/b]. You keep the pot, and you don't need a table or a fire.
description=[*][b]Cook the Raw Soap[/b] for 60 minutes in an oven, on a campfire, barbecue or fireplace: each one turns into a full bar of soap.
description=[/olist]
description=Everyone knows Trim Fat. Mix Soap has to be learned.
description=
description=[h2]Learning Mix Soap[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Read [b]"Fight Club" by Chuck Palahniuk[/b]. It turns up wherever fiction books spawn.
description=[*]Read [b]Soapmaker's Guide to Galaxy[/b], a cooking magazine that turns up wherever Good Cooking magazines spawn.
description=[*]Or start as a [b]Keen Cook[/b] (Chef and Burger Flipper included), [b]Bushcrafter[/b], [b]Rancher[/b] or [b]Farmer[/b].
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Good to know[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Build 42, single player and multiplayer. The server and every player need the mod.
description=[*]Lua and scripts only, no Java.
description=[*]Plays nicely with other trait mods: the recipe is added to traits and professions while the game runs, without replacing their definitions.
description=[*]Characters made before you added the mod don't get the recipe from their trait. They can still read the book or the magazine.
description=[*]English and Polish.
description=[/list]
description=
